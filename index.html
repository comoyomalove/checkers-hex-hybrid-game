How to play

Connect any two opposite sides of the board with an unbroken line of your pieces.

The board has four sides:
  • Top and bottom — marked with a green border
  • Left and right — marked with a red border
You win the moment your pieces form a continuous path from one side to the directly opposite side. The path can bend and zigzag in any direction — it does not have to be a straight line.

Either player can win across either axis. No sides are assigned.
Players alternate turns. On each turn, one of two things happens:

  • If you have a piece that can capture — you must capture. You cannot place freely.
  • If no capture is available — click any empty cell on the board to place your piece there.
Pieces never move after being placed — the only exception is a capture jump.
When no capture is available, click any empty cell to place your piece.

You may place next to your own pieces, next to opponent pieces, or anywhere on the board. There are no restricted zones.

If your placement creates a capture opportunity for your piece, the capture resolves immediately and automatically during the same turn.
A capture is a jump. Your piece jumps over an opponent's piece and lands on the empty cell directly beyond it. The opponent's piece is removed.

For a capture to be legal, all of these must be true:
  • Your piece is on the board
  • An opponent's piece is directly adjacent along a diagonal direction
  • The cell on the far side of the opponent's piece is empty
You cannot capture sideways (East ↔ West). Only diagonal directions count for capture.
If any of your pieces can capture at the start of your turn, you must capture. You cannot place a new piece instead.

How to capture step by step:
  • Pieces that must capture are highlighted purple
  • Click a purple piece to select it
  • Valid landing cells appear in bright green — click one to jump
  • The selected piece turns cyan while you choose
  • If you change your mind, click a different purple piece to re-select
If multiple captures are available, you may choose which one to take — but you must take one.
After a capture, if your piece can jump again from its new position, you must continue jumping.

The chain ends only when no further jump is available from the landing cell. Your turn ends at that point.

During a chain, green landing cells appear after each jump — keep clicking until the chain ends.
On this hex board, captures are only legal along two diagonal axes:
  • Northeast ↔ Southwest
  • Northwest ↔ Southeast
The East ↔ West direction is not a capture direction.

All six hex directions still count for connection — your winning path can travel in any direction.
After a game (or during one), use the Replay controls to review every move:

  • ▶ Play — auto-plays through all turns
  • ⏸ Pause — stops auto-play
  • ← Prev Turn — step back one turn
  • Next Turn → — step forward one turn
If you go back and make a different move, the game records it as a fork — a new branch from that point. All branches are saved.
